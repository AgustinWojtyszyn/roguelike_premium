RPG NEW — ORIGINAL MUSIC

The in-game music is synthesized by scripts/core/original_music_library.gd.

Cues:
- refuge: calm menu/refuge loop
- dungeon: exploration/combat loop
- warden: boss loop

The tracks use no third-party recordings, samples, vocals, stems or imported melodies.
They are generated once per cue as small 8-bit mono PCM buffers at 11025 Hz and then cached.
Playback does not synthesize audio per frame, keeping runtime CPU cost intentionally low for Android.

The existing sound effects under assets/audio/kenney remain CC0 and keep their original license files.
