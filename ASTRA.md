# Astra instructions

Follow `AGENTS.md` and execute the **Astra — art/game-feel pass** in `docs/CLAUDE_ASTRA_ASSET_BRIEF.md`.

Do not start by converting the entire source bank. Review Claude's vertical slice first. Keep only assets that materially raise the visual bar.

Priorities:
- one coherent 3/4 orthographic camera and scale across every render;
- strong readable silhouettes at Android gameplay size;
- real weapon-holding poses and believable grip;
- consistent outline, light direction, shadow and palette;
- compact, dense dungeon composition rather than large empty rooms;
- reject generic-looking source assets even when technically valid;
- preserve 60 FPS target and the existing 2D runtime.

Do not restore the previous Rpg_new playable sheets or the procedural polygon player. Do not merge to main automatically.
